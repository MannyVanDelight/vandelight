Prompts for everyday work

Copy a prompt into your approved AI tool and replace the highlighted parts with your own details. Open each one to see what you'll get and what to check.

    Use AI safely

    Before you paste anything, sort it into one of these three groups.

    Usually fine

    • Your own rough notes and drafts
    • General questions and how-tos
    • Public information
    • Templates you'd share with any colleague

    Remove details first

    • Names of people, clients and suppliers
    • Project names and addresses
    • Rates and prices, if your policy allows them at all

    Keep out

    • Personal details such as health, HR or contact information
    • Passwords and access details
    • Anything under a confidentiality agreement
    • Incident details that identify a person

    This is a general guide. Your organisation's AI policy always comes first.

    Run a 30-minute session

    A plan any AI champion can run with a team of six to twelve people. No slides needed.

    You'll need: a shared screen, the approved AI tool open, this kit, and two or three tasks the team does every week that involve no sensitive data.

    1. 0–5minutes

      Start with their work

      Ask what eats the team's time each week. Write three answers where everyone can see them.

    2. 5–12minutes

      Show one live example

      Run a prompt from this kit on one of those tasks. Point out something the AI got wrong and how you caught it.

    3. 12–22minutes

      Try it in pairs

      Each pair picks a prompt for their role and runs it on a real, non-sensitive task.

    4. 22–27minutes

      Share what happened

      Each pair says what worked and what they had to fix.

    5. 27–30minutes

      Safe use and one next step

      Walk through the Safe use checklist. Ask everyone to name one task they'll try this week.

    Tips for the facilitator

    • Let the AI make a mistake in the demo. It builds trust faster than a perfect run.
    • Use the team's own words for their tasks, not AI jargon.
    • Note every use case people mention and pass it to your AI team, so it can become a new prompt in this kit.

    These prompts work in most chat-based AI tools. Always follow your organisation's AI policy.
