The Art of Not Crumbling Under Pressure

Umami Origami

AboutA high-fashion dress built entirely from paper, shot across real landscapes, with 3D-rendered origami butterflies added in post.
TechniqueHandcrafted paper couture, on-location photography, VFX compositing, and one AI-generated companion piece.
ThemeOr, how I metaphorically and literally glued a project together.

Hello, fellow rascals.

There is a quiet pressure in the creative world to keep things tidy. To propose concepts that fit neatly into predictable boxes, follow the path of least resistance, and guarantee a smooth, frictionless production. It's safe, it's comfortable, and it is the fastest way to make sure a piece of work leaves absolutely no impression at all.

Personally, I've always found that the most compelling visions live right on the edge of a total logistical collapse.

Today, I'm taking you behind the scenes of my latest project: Umami Origami. It's a project born from a simple, slightly unhinged premise: what happens when you mix high fashion, structural engineering challenges, elegant VFX, a dash of AI, and a severe lack of respect for the weather forecast?

Spoiler alert: you get magic. But first, you get a lot of stress.

The Main Event

The concept was simple: craft a high-fashion dress entirely out of paper, shoot it across breathtaking landscapes, and add 3D-rendered origami butterflies in post-production. Easy on paper. Not as straightforward in reality.

The Real-World Struggle (IRL Costs Real Money)

Paper hates the outdoors.

First of all, finding a dressmaker who doesn't immediately throw scissors at you when you say "make it out of paper" is a challenge. Plus it cost a pretty penny to bring to life, and once we got it on set, the universe decided to test my sanity.

Wind

Every gentle breeze threatened to violently fold the neck piece into oblivion.

Humidity

Dampened the paper, making the entire gown heavy, fragile, and prone to melting.

I had to pull out some serious McGyver moves, reinforcing the structural integrity of the dress mid-shoot while praying to the photography gods it wouldn't show in the final frames. Then there was the model. Bless her soul. Sitting down, moving, or doing literally anything came with a high risk of catastrophic structural failure. But we survived. And the results speak for themselves.

Cheating Geography via VFX

If you look at the locations, you'll notice they span across New Zealand and Australia. Did I fly the whole crew to every remote peak? Absolutely not — I value my bank account.

For a couple of these concepts I leaned heavily into my visual effects background: shot the locations solo, shot the model separately, and merged her into the environments seamlessly. The 3D-rendered origami butterflies aren't pasted on top; they cast accurate shadows, catch the ambient light, and perfectly mimic the colours of her paper dress. To tie the physical world together, I also captured two shots featuring actual fabric dresses, injecting the digital butterflies to blur the line between the ordinary and the extraordinary.

Enter the Auramancer (The AI Experiment)

The Gold Dancer.

Because the physical paper dress tried to murder my workflow, I decided to see how the digital gods would handle the pressure. I wanted to design an even more extreme dress for a ballet dancer.

If you replace "glass" with "gold" in the word Vitrumancer, you get Auramancer (Aurum = Latin for gold, Mancer = one with control over an element). A Gold Dancer. Instead of spending weeks and a small fortune on a physical dress that would probably break a dancer's legs, I trained AI on exactly what I wanted. It was the only AI contribution to the project, but it proved a point: for scale, speed, and versatility, embracing the machine let me realize a concept that would otherwise be locked behind a budget wall.

The Peak — Midnight Magic

Midnight Magic delves deep into the myriad emotions and experiences that come with immigrating to a new country. At its heart, it is a visual narrative of transformation, resilience, and growth, as one adapts to new circumstances.

The art style is divided into distinct elements, each symbolizing a phase of this profound journey. On one hand, there is the night, black and white — the melancholy of leaving one's roots behind, a teary-eyed farewell to family and friends. On the other, the heavy tone is balanced with light-hearted magic: the moon lighting the way, sparkling mist, glowing creatures welcoming us to the new land.

It's an immigrant's transformative odyssey — cultural shocks, bittersweet nostalgia, the forging of new bonds, and beauty discovered in the most unexpected times. A piece about the universal human experience of migration, and the ever-unfolding tapestry of cultures that make our world so rich.

The best work isn't born in a sterile environment where everything goes according to plan. It's born when the paper dress is tearing, the wind is blowing, the locations are thousands of miles apart, and you are forced to use every tool in your arsenal — from VFX to AI to literal sticky tape — to make the vision reality.I am not afraid to break things to make something beautiful.

Stay delightfully unusual,
Manny Van Delight — Creative Director / Visual Alchemist

Mooning The Ignorant

AboutNine looping animations riffing on the quirks, rituals, and copium of the crypto space.
Technique3D animation loops, built for a wall, minted on-chain.
ThemeFilm history and blockchain culture, in the same frame.

This project is all about the quirks and features of the crypto space — but it also combines that with my actual passion, which is film and filmmaking. Somewhere between a group chat in-joke and a film studies essay, "Mooning The Ignorant" was born.

When Moon is a direct quote and homage to Georges Méliès' iconic 1902 silent sci-fi film A Trip to the Moon. Blockchain, much like a rocket in the eye of the moon, is a fairly ridiculous idea that happened anyway — and it acts as the foundation every other piece in this collection stands on.

Nine loops. Watch on repeat, laugh a little, maybe recognize yourself in one or two. No advice given, some feelings implied.

Interactive Filmmaking & Community Worldbuilding

Shattered Dawn

AboutAn interactive sci-fi universe where the audience steps into the director's chair to vote, shape the plot, and forge the destiny of our heroine, Layla.
TechniqueCinematic VFX compositing, AI concept art wrestling, 3D prop modeling, interactive polling, and Bitcoin Ordinals integration.
ThemeWho needs a predictable Hollywood script when you can hand the steering wheel to a horde of passionate internet rascals?

Listen up, fellow rascals.

For decades, traditional filmmaking has treated audiences like passive passengers: you sit in a dark room, eat your overpriced popcorn, and watch whatever pre-packaged story a studio executive greenlit three years ago. If a character makes a mind-bogglingly bad decision, you scream at the screen, but nothing changes.

I got tired of yelling at the screen. So I thought: What happens if we hand the steering wheel directly to the audience?

Welcome to Shattered Dawn — my ongoing sandbox experiment in interactive, community-driven sci-fi filmmaking. It is equal parts cinematic worldbuilding, high-stakes visual effects, relentless AI prompting battles, and raw community democracy.

No safety net. No script. Just Layla, the crowd, and whatever chaos we vote into existence.

The Core Premise — Vote Your Heroine

At its heart, Shattered Dawn follows our protagonist, Layla, as she navigates a treacherous dystopian world filled with toxic atmosphere, illegal technology, and rogue factions. But here's the twist: you call the shots.

Through community voting rounds, fans dictate critical narrative forks: from Layla's tactical maneuvers and alliance allegiances down to the literal gear and weapons she crafts to survive. Every single vote bends the trajectory of the storyline, turning story creation into a real-time multiplayer experience.

Wrestling the Digital Gods (VFX + AI Chaos)

When MidJourney decides every prompt is a suggestion, not an instruction.

Bringing a community-voted story to life at feature-film visual standards in real-time is a glorious, chaotic challenge. Case in point: when the community voted on designing "The Extractor" — a crucial piece of high-tech gear for Layla — I turned to AI tools to iterate fast concepts.

Prompting Mayhem

MidJourney kept hallucinating sci-fi space-toasters and alien vacuum cleaners instead of sleek extraction tools.

VFX Refinement

Taking raw concepts into 3D software and Photoshop to carve out crisp, vote-ready assets fitting cinematic standards.

I ended up pulling back the curtain in behind-the-scenes vlogs, showing the unvarnished battle between human creative direction and artificial intelligence gone rogue until we achieved three clean options for the community poll. That's the heartbeat of this project: it's transparent, unpredictable, and delightfully unhinged.

On-Chain Lore & Interactive Ordinals

To take co-creation even deeper, Shattered Dawn weaves interactive digital collectibles and Bitcoin Ordinals into the mix. Owning a piece of the universe isn't just about holding digital art; it acts as a pass to influence key story arcs and lock your mark into the official canon forever.

Shattered Dawn isn't just a film you sit back and watch. It's a living universe we build together — one vote, one render, and one wild plot twist at a time.Democracy has arrived in cinema, and it carries high-voltage VFX.

Ready to shape the dawn?
Manny Van Delight — Visual Alchemist & Storyteller
